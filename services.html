What We Do

Three domains.

They map to Automate, Delegate and Accelerate, and they run in that order for a reason. Skip the first and the other two work against you.

01 · AUTOMATE

Workflow

Milestones, tasks and triggers built against your SOPs, job type by job type. Every step of every shipment mapped, sequenced and running the same way each time — plus the ongoing configuration work as new job types, customers and lanes come on board.

This is the foundation for everything else. Where the configuration is not there yet, AI agents amplify the problems rather than solve them.

02 · DELEGATE

AI Agents

CargoWise ships new agent capability with every Value Pack. We assess whether your operation is ready for a given feature, configure the workflow underneath it, and get it running properly — commercial invoice parsing, compliance screening, classification.

Then we set the checkpoints where a human still signs off. Statutory and financial responsibility does not move to an agent.

Current CargoWise AI features →
03 · ACCELERATE

PAVE

Visual boards, buffer management and performance measurement configured for your operation. PAVE makes the work visible, surfaces the constraint, and keeps the right job in front of the right person — including current task load against available capacity.

Buffer signals come with a defined response — watch, plan, act — so a red board is not left to interpretation. We tune it as you grow. PAVE is not a set-and-forget system.

Training

Skills that change how your team works.

Configuration alone does not hold. Every step of Automate, Delegate and Accelerate makes higher demands on human judgement, and that is trainable.

Black Belt in Thinking

Automating requires people who understand their own workflows. Delegating requires people who know when an agent has got it wrong and why. Managing escalations under pressure requires rapid cause-and-effect reasoning and the ability to decide fast without second-guessing.

BBIT is that training — the same thinking discipline PAVE is built on, applied to the people who run the operation. Not a standalone personal development course. The prerequisite for the rest of the operation working as designed.

Visit blackbeltinthinking.com →

PAVE Certification

Three levels, each building on the last. PCO gives operators the foundation — they know which job to work on next and why. PCS gives team leaders the systems view, managing flow across the whole operation. PCP gives operations managers the tools to design, tune and maintain the PAVE system as the business changes.
