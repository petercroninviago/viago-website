The Problem

Changes made on the fly.

Mainfreight's own account: like most freight forwarders they had always sought out ways to improve the efficiency of their operations, and saw that as a continuing process that would never end. The difficulty was that they could often fall into making process changes on the fly, because of a shipment being missed or a service failure that should not have happened.

Going in, they named four things they wanted the productivity tools to address:

  • Complete jobs in a timely fashion and in the correct order.
  • Highlight problem jobs before they became problems.
  • Evenly share workloads.
  • Highlight bottlenecks that negatively affect other areas of the business.
The Work

With the teams, not at them.

The CargoWise One Productivity Suite was implemented across the Mainfreight Air & Ocean Australia operation. What it offered, in Mainfreight's words, was visualisation of where shipments were in the process life-cycle and the ability to see possible problems before they escalated.

Mainfreight staff were engaged in the planning and implementation rather than handed a finished system. That let their teams sit down and analyse the full process, and start to fully understand the dependencies that different areas had on each other.

Building a workflow that covers the life-cycle of a freight movement handled by multiple areas of the business helped smooth the workload and avoid large peaks and troughs.

Like most freight forwarders, Mainfreight has always sought out ways to improve the efficiency of our operations.
Robert CotterBusiness Solutions and Process Manager, Mainfreight
What Changed

Five KPIs, all up.

  • Mainfreight measured against five key internal KPIs. The increase on each was substantial. They were careful to say the improvement was not totally due to CargoWise One, but that the increases correlated to when the productivity tools were fully implemented.
  • Workload smoothed out, with fewer large peaks and troughs.
  • Greater communication between areas, once teams understood the dependencies between them.
  • An unexpected but welcome benefit: engaging the team in the planning and implementation gave people an opportunity to shine, and gave Mainfreight confidence that their next group of leaders was coming through. Their daily productivity stand-up meetings continue to highlight it.
