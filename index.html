How We Work

Automate. Delegate. Accelerate.

Three moves that change what your operation can carry — and who has to carry it.

01

Automate

Milestones, tasks and triggers configured to match your SOPs. Every step of every shipment mapped, sequenced and ready to run consistently.

02

Delegate

Tasks are delegated to AI agents to handle the predictable, business-as-usual work. High touch, high volume, low complexity handled without your people touching it.

03

Accelerate

When agents handle the routine, your team's job changes. Reviews and escalations become the whole job. When volumes spike, so does the pressure. PAVE buffers that surge and keeps the right work in front of the right person.

PAVE · The Workflow Engine

The right work, in front of the right person.

Agents clear the routine. PAVE governs what's left — sequencing every job by priority and buffer position so nothing important slips and no one is guessing what to do next.

  • Visual boards — every job's status and priority, live. Operators stop guessing; the board tells them where they're needed.
  • Buffer management — green, amber and red surface risk while there's still time to act.
  • Escalation to a human — the exceptions agents can't resolve reach a person, fast, with the context to decide.
