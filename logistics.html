The Future of Freight Forwarding Operations

Three types of work. Very different demands on your people.

In a well-configured CargoWise operation, work splits into three distinct streams, and the third is where your operation either holds together or falls apart.

AGENTS

The routine, handled

Predictable, high-volume, low-complexity tasks: document processing, compliance screening, tariff classification, all done without an operator touching them.

SCHEDULED REVIEWS

Human checkpoints

Defined moments where a person signs off: customs lodgement, billing sign-off, anything with statutory or financial weight.

ESCALATIONS

The judgement calls

Unexpected exceptions agents couldn't resolve, surfaced to a human who needs to make a fast, informed decision. This is where the pressure lands.

1Automate

Get the workflow right before anything else.

CargoWise's workflow engine maps every job type as milestones, tasks and triggers. Configured properly against your SOPs, every step of every shipment runs consistently. When the configuration is not there yet, AI agents amplify the problems rather than solve them. This is where ViAGO lays the groundwork.

01

Milestones

The defined stages every job moves through. Origin, docs received, customs lodged, cleared, delivered, billed. Every job type has its own milestone sequence, configured to match your SOPs.

02

Tasks

The work items attached to each milestone. Who does what, in what order, before the job can progress. Tasks make the work visible and assignable without anyone having to ask what comes next.

03

Triggers

The automation layer. When an event fires in CargoWise (a milestone completed, a document received, a deadline hit), triggers send notifications, generate documents and route tasks automatically. Day or night.

2Delegate

Hand the routine work to agents.

Once the operational process is dialled in, AI agents take on the high-touch, high-volume, low-complexity work. The agents that do this are built into CargoWise today. Getting them ready to perform requires the workflow foundation from Step 1 to already be in place.

3Accelerate

PAVE manages what is left: the work that needs a human.

When agents handle the routine, human work changes. Reviews and escalations become the whole job. Volume is unpredictable, priorities shift fast, and the pressure to act on the right thing at the right time is higher than ever. PAVE gives operators a real-time view of every job's status, priority and buffer position. Managers see risk before it becomes a service failure.

01

Visual Boards

Real-time view of every job's status and priority. The primary operator interface. Operators stop guessing what to work on next. The board tells them, based on where the system needs them most.

02

Buffer Management

Governs when work is released and sequenced through operations. Green, amber and red zones surface risk early, while there is still time to act rather than after the damage is done.

03

Performance Measurement

KPI dashboards give managers a factual view of system health. Throughput, flow rates, exception patterns. The data to improve the operation systematically, not by instinct.
